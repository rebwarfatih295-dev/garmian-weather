Privacy Policy — Garmiyan Weather (کەشوهەوای گەرمیان)

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Developer: Rebwar Manj Ali (ڕێبوار مانج علی)

1. Information Collection and Use

Garmiyan Weather ("کەشوهەوای گەرمیان") does not collect, store, or share any personal user data, location tracking history, or device identifiers. All weather forecasts are retrieved directly using fixed geographic coordinates for the cities and districts of the Garmiyan Administration.

2. Third-Party Services

The app fetches real-time meteorological data from the Open-Meteo public weather API. No personally identifiable information is transmitted in these requests.
